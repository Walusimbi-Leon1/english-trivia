## Long Description

(Discord limit is 1024 characters — this is 784)

> English Trivia is a fast-paced multiplayer trivia game about the English language. Every 20 seconds, a new question appears — idioms, proverbs, colloquialisms, jargon, euphemisms, maxims, and clichés.
>
> Everyone in your voice channel sees the same question at the same time. Answer fast to score big: the first correct answer in each round earns the most points, and speed rewards drop off the longer you wait. Climb the shared leaderboard, keep your rank across sessions, and challenge friends in Discord Activities or directly in your browser.
>
> Questions come from a growing open-source corpus of real English phrases, refreshed continuously by an automated pipeline — so the game never runs dry. Free to play, no downloads, no sign-up required. Just launch the activity and play!
